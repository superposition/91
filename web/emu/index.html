Optional MIDI controllers and FM-1 remote mode

Play it from your FM-1, or any MIDI controller

Plug the FM-1 in by USB and press Connect MIDI (Chrome, Edge or Firefox; Safari has no Web MIDI). With NAIWAN 91 on the FM-1, press SEL + GLO on it: its panel then plays this emulator exactly (buttons, the 27 keys, every knob and MASTER), its own sound goes quiet, its screen says REMOTE, and its lights follow the screen here. SEL + GLO again gives it back, stopped. (It speaks MIDI channel 16: keys are notes 48 up, buttons CC 102 up, knobs CC 85 up, MASTER CC 7; the lights come back through the FM-1's own MIDI output, which the browser has to see under the same name.)

With M-VAVE's firmware, or any other keyboard or controller, its notes play the keys here straight away (any channel, folded into F3 to G5). For the rest use MIDI learn: press MIDI learn, click a knob, button or key on the panel, then move or press the control you want on your controller. A knob needs a knob or slider (a CC); a button or key takes a note or a CC (64 and over is down). A learned knob can be absolute or relative (64 ± n, or 1 up and 127 down): pick it in the list below, which also lets you forget one. What you learn is kept in this browser. MIDI clock, SysEx and program changes are ignored.

Your map on the FM-1 itself (experimental): what you learn here can play the real NAIWAN 91 too. Press Send it to your FM-1 (the Install page's MIDI map section, in this browser) and your controller plays the FM-1's buttons, keys and knobs with no computer in between. Download map saves it as a .perfmap file to share or keep; Load map reads one.

Nothing learns? Your controller may keep its pads off MIDI. On an MPC Sample: SHIFT + pad 8 (MIDI Config), MIDI Port USB, Pad MIDI Out Always. While learning, anything heard shows above.

    Choose the band

    PRESETS chooses Blue Bossa, Autumn Leaves or Satin Doll. HOME returns to the Songbook performance screen.

    Steer the phrase

    The four main knobs set key, tempo, section length and movement. ALGORITHM adjusts energy. Key and form changes wait for a section boundary.

    Shape the voices

    SELECT opens Arrangement, DX Keys, DX Bass and Swells. On the two DX pages, knob 1 chooses a program and knob 2 sets that part's octave independently.

    Hold, vary and keep

    F3 / REC holds a section. G3 / EDIT requests a new bass motif. SAVE stores session settings while playback is stopped. New start restores what you saved.

    Mouse, touch and keyboard

    • Drag a knob up or down, or use the scroll wheel over it. Click or touch buttons and keys (several fingers at once work); Shift-click (or right-click) a button to hold it down, click again to let go.
    • White keys 1–8: Q–I, 9–16: A–K. Black keys 1–10: 1–0, black key 11: -.
    • Space PLAY, Enter REC, Esc HOME, V ENV, L LFO, P ARP, M SEQ, O GLO, C EDIT, ` FX, B SAVE, N SEL, Z / X OCT− / OCT+. A held key is a held button, so hold M for the sequencer or N with another key for SEL combinations.
    • ↑ ↓ turn SELECT. The other knobs (ALGORITHM, PRESETS, KNOB 1–4, MASTER) have no letter: use the mouse, or press Tab to a knob and turn it with the arrow keys.
    • Hover a control for a one-line tip. Stream view (a button under the panel, or ?stream on the address) shows the FM-1 and its screen large on a 16:9 stage for capturing.

    Pick up where you left off

    A new browser starts with Blue Bossa. Stop playback and press SAVE to keep your song, key, tempo and section settings here. New start returns to your saved session. Forget stored clears your saved songs and settings from this browser.

    More sounds to explore

    F4 opens generative pieces, sequencers, effects and visualizers. The manual walks through those controls.

    Leave room for your part

    Set a comfortable tempo and let the accompaniment play while you try a melody. Hold a phrase for another pass, change the bass feel or bring in a swell when you want the music to open up.
