A small instrument.
A band to play with.

Choose a song, set your key and tempo, and play along. NAIWAN 91 brings moving chords, electric keys, a rounded bass and gentle swells. Hold a section while you find a melody, then ask the band for a new idea.

No FM-1 required. Press Switch on when you’re ready to hear it.

Play a song, then shape it

Start simply. Find your sound as you go.

Three original songs

Velvet Changes, Afterhours and Neon Turnaround each have their own electric-piano character, bass feel and harmonic journey. Turn PRESETS to choose one.

Keys, bass and swells

Soften the keys, brighten their attack, give the bass more space or bring in a swell. SELECT opens four pages of controls.

Keep the phrase alive

Hold a section, request a related idea or change the bass feel. Key and form changes land at the end of a phrase, keeping the music moving.

Make it yours

Four pages for the arrangement and the sound of your band.

Leave room for your part

Bring an instrument, try a melody, or settle in and listen.

Find your starting point

  • Choose a song with PRESETS
  • Set a comfortable key and tempo
  • Choose an eight- or sixteen-bar section
  • Let the accompaniment play while you find your part

Try something new

  • Hold a phrase for another pass
  • Ask for a related variation
  • Change the bass feel
  • Save your session when you stop

Use it on an FM-1

Hardware is optional for the browser instrument.

Install and back up

The USB-MIDI installer includes saved-object backup, restoration and recovery tools. Read the hardware instructions before installing.

Find your sound

Connect an FM-1 to adjust sounds and edit DX voices. Use the Songbook pages to choose the sound and feel of the keys, bass and swells.

Explore the controls

There are more generative pieces, sequencers, effects and visualizers to explore. The manual walks through the controls.
