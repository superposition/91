A band to play with.

Choose a standard, set your key and tempo, and play along. Warm electric keys, bright brass, soft pads and rounded bass give familiar changes a different feel. Let the band play, or take the keys yourself with Synth Jam.

Play a song, then shape it

Start simply. Find your sound as you go.

Start with a standard

Blue Bossa, Autumn Leaves and Satin Doll have their own changes, grooves and sounds. Turn PRESETS to choose one. The accompaniment follows the written harmony.

Find your band's sound

Each song brings six keyboard sounds and two bass sounds. SELECT opens the sound pages: choose a program, lift the keys an octave or drop the bass lower. A spacious plate gives each part room.

Take the keyboard part

Open Synth Jam and turn MODE to SYNTH. Play your own chords and lines while drums and bass carry on. HOME brings the band back without restarting the song.

Make it yours

Four pages for the arrangement and the sound of your band.

Leave room for your part

Bring an instrument, try a melody, or settle in and listen.

Find your starting point

  • Choose a song with PRESETS
  • Set a comfortable key and tempo
  • Play the full chart or loop a shorter section
  • Let the accompaniment play while you find your part

Try something new

  • Hold a section for another pass
  • Try close chords, drop-2 or an inversion
  • Play a line against the bass in Synth Jam
  • Change the sound, octave or space as you play

Install and back up

The USB-MIDI installer includes saved-object backup, restoration and recovery tools. Read the hardware instructions before installing.

Make it your band

Use the Songbook pages for these arrangements, or load your own chart and sounds into a user pack. The connected editor also supports legacy parameters and DX voices.

Explore the controls

There are more generative pieces, sequencers, effects and visualizers to explore. The manual walks through the controls.
